About the data
  • Each ring is the average fall shoreline (Sep 1 – Nov 15), traced from cloud-free Landsat images.
  • Healthy range starts at 4,198 ft (Utah DNR). Lake level from the USGS Saltair gauge.
  • Water needed comes from USGS lakebed volume tables. No outline for 2025.
  • Sources: USGS Landsat via Esri Living Atlas, processed in ArcGIS Pro; USGS lake levels.
