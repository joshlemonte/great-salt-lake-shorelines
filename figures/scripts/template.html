Each ring is that year's typical shoreline from September 1 to November 15, classified from every clear Landsat look (MNDWI with a per-scene Otsu threshold, cloud masked pixel by pixel). Dashed rings and open circles mark years where cloud gaps touch more than 10% of the shoreline.

Farmington and Bear River bays are included only while connected to the main lake. The 1987 lobe on the west side is flooded low ground; it is cut where it ran past the western edge of the area searched.
